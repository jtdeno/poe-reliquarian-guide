Gear, armour and flasks

The Hardcore order of operations: links → life → capped resistances → useful base defenses → damage upgrades.

Weapon setup

Wand or sceptre + shield

Both mine skills are spells, so raw weapon attack DPS is irrelevant. A shield adds life, resistances and defenses.

  1. For Stormblast: spell damage and lightning spell levels.
  2. For Pyroclast: +1 fire spell gems, spell/fire damage, added fire to spells.
  3. Mine throwing speed matters; cast speed does not speed up mine throwing.
Armour choice

Armour/Evasion or Evasion/ES

Both are good. Armour/Evasion helps build physical mitigation and attack avoidance. Evasion/ES offers a buffer and useful suppression-capable bases.

While leveling: choose the stronger life + resistance + linked-socket item, regardless of base type.

White sockets are the normal 3.29+ system. Don't spend currency fixing socket colors for usability.

Reliquarian Labyrinth plan

The build's major identity comes online later. Do not rush dangerous labs with poor life or uncapped resistances.

Normal · Area level 33

Two Passive Point nodes

Reliquarian's first Labyrinth does not grant a unique-derived effect. Invest the points into regular passives.

Cruel · Area level 55

The Brass Dome

+5% maximum elemental resistances and no extra damage taken from critical strikes. Strength stops granting its usual maximum-life bonus. You must actually gear up to your raised resistance cap.

Merciless · Area level 68

Foulborn Tremor Rod

Mines can be detonated an additional time and have 50% increased effect of auras from mines. Main mechanical power spike for our Pyroclast strategy.

Eternal · Area level 75

The Arkhon's Tools

Skitterbots gain Scorch and enhanced non-damaging ailment effect. Only rush this if your gear and defenses justify the risk.
