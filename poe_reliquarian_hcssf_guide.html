Overview

The Ossuary · Essence Drain Lich

A full-life, energy-shield-first Witch built to stay mobile while damage ticks. Contagion proliferates Essence Drain through dense Ritual packs; Dark Effigy supplies boss DPS from range. No chase unique, trade, CI, low-life setup, or self-sacrifice is required.

HCSSF rule: Never plant yourself to cast through a telegraph. If energy shield bottoms out, disengage and recharge. Eternal Life is not immunity when ES is gone.

Rotation

  1. Pack: Contagion → Essence Drain on a weak enemy → move while the debuffs spread.
  2. Rare/boss: Despair → Contagion → ED → Dark Effigy → retreat and refresh as needed. Withering Touch supports the totem's hits; Decaying Hex introduces another debuff.
  3. Reposition before recasting, and remember damage over time continues while you move.

Why Forbidden Rites?

0.5.5 retains the preceding skill balance but puts Ritual in campaign zones, creating more chances at useful rares, gems and currency. Use the high-density mechanic only when the area and modifiers are safe. The Trial of Chaos now rewards each room, and can be paused: stop before a dangerous room rather than chasing a reward.

Cost: Both supports increase mana cost. Lifetap (level 8+) is an alternative if mana would otherwise block an escape, but it spends life and prevents that skill from generating Arcane Surge through mana spending. For HC, test the life cost before relying on it.

Early lightning help · Orb of Storms level 4+

Orb of Storms · optional early damage

Orb of Storms+Added Lightning Damage

Start: Orb of Storms alone. Add Added Lightning Damage from level 8 if you have a spare copy and sockets; keep your mine support first.

Later option: Power Charge On Critical (level 18+) can generate charges from the orb’s own critical hits if you deliberately build around them. It is not required for this leveling setup.

Priority: This is temporary supplemental damage. Drop the orb when its button and sockets stop earning their place after the Pyroclast switch. Do not force a large utility damage link at the expense of defenses.

Chill, shock & mine damage · level 16+

Summon Skitterbots · 1-link first

Summon Skitterbots

Start: No support required. Base reservation is 35% mana.

Helpful later: Link Unbound Ailments (level 8+ support) to strengthen the bots’ chill and shock. This is an optional upgrade once your mana budget allows it.

Reservation: Its 120% multiplier raises base Skitterbots reservation from 35% to 42% before efficiency modifiers and rounding. Test your full boss mine sequence before keeping the link. Minion damage supports are not the goal for these bots.

Flammability+Faster Casting

Start: Flammability alone once using fire damage. Faster Casting is optional for spending less time standing still to apply it.

Helpful from level 31: Add or use More Duration to reduce how often you need to refresh the curse. This is the current gem name; older guides may call it Increased Duration.

Cost: Extra supports raise the cast cost. Keep a cheap manual curse if mana is tight. Turning it into a Blasphemy aura adds reservation and is not the default for this mine setup.

Defensive aura · level 24+

Purity of Elements · leave unsupported

Purity of Elements

Start: No support required. Base reservation is 50% mana for elemental resistances and elemental ailment immunity.

Late SSF option: Link Enlighten at gem level 2+ if you find and level one. Level 1 Enlighten gives no reservation reduction; this build must function without finding it.

Link hygiene: Keep this aura away from compatible supports that increase reservation without helping its purpose. Adding supports does not automatically improve your resistance bonus. Choose your aura setup around the defenses and free mana you actually have.

Optional boss debuff · Bear Trap level 4+

Bear Trap · duration over damage

Bear Trap+More Duration

Start: Bear Trap alone. Its debuff makes the affected enemy take increased damage from traps and mines; its own hit is secondary here.

Helpful from level 31: More Duration extends the debuff, leaving more time to move and detonate mines between refreshes.

Cost: Keep the active gem at an affordable level for its utility role. Add the duration support only when you can spare the socket and mana. This is optional boss utility, not another mandatory damage link.

Guard for low-armour gear · Steelskin level 4+

Steelskin · manual protection first

Steelskin+More Duration

Start: Steelskin alone, used before a dangerous hit. Add More Duration from level 31 for a longer protection window; the guard can still be depleted early.

Optional from level 38: Cast when Damage Taken + Steelskin + More Duration. Keep the active gem’s required character level within the trigger support’s limit. Do not blindly level either gem.

HC tradeoff: CWDT only triggers after damage is taken and cannot protect against the hit that first activates it. Triggered casts still need their resource cost. Higher trigger thresholds can leave long gaps; manual use gives control over timing.

Guard for substantial armour · Molten Shell level 16+

Molten Shell · armour-dependent option

Molten Shell+More Duration

Choose instead of Steelskin: Molten Shell’s absorption scales with armour. Compare its actual protection in PoB before switching; a small amount of armour alone is not a reason to use it.

Helpful from level 31: More Duration. From level 38, Cast when Damage Taken + Molten Shell + More Duration is an optional trigger setup, with the same level-limit and resource checks as Steelskin.

Shared cooldown: Guard skills share cooldowns. Use one planned guard setup. Keep a manually used guard out of your Automation links so it does not fire early and spend the cooldown before danger arrives.

Mana and socket priorities

Reserve an escape budget: Purity of Elements + unsupported Skitterbots reserve 85% of mana before modifiers. With Unbound Ailments on Skitterbots, that becomes 92%. Neither combination is automatically usable: mines also reserve mana, and movement, curses and guards need resources.

Before entering a boss fight, check that you can place your intended mine stack, cast your utility skills and still Flame Dash away. If you cannot, remove an optional support or reconsider the aura combination. For a leveling character, unsupported auras and a simple movement link are often the right setup.

Guard triggers: Choose manual use, CWDT, or a deliberate Automation setup; do not link two trigger supports to the same guard. Faster Casting is not a useful link for instant guard skills.

Soft targets, not fake guarantees

Through campaign: capped elemental resistance and appropriately leveled life/mana flasks. Near white maps, aim for roughly 3,500–4,000 life, meaningful unreserved mana and mana regeneration, with chaos resistance moving toward non-negative. These are safety benchmarks, not stats copied from Maxroll.

MoM math: with 40% of damage taken from mana before life, a mana pool around two-thirds of your life is the rough capacity-matching benchmark. It is not enough by itself: totem, movement and guard costs plus mana regeneration matter.
